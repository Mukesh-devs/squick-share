How it worksWith Android phones and Windows PCs

Share both ways, in three steps.

From an Android phone to your Mac

  1. Step 1

    Share from your phone.

    On your Android phone, tap Share on any photo, video or file, then Quick Share, and pick your Mac.

From your Mac to an Android phone

  1. Step 1

    Get your phone ready.

    On the phone, open Quick Share and tap Receive, or use the Quick Share tile in Quick Settings. Keep it open while you send.

From a Windows PC to your Mac

  1. Step 1

    Keep your Mac visible.

    SQuick Share shows your Mac to everyone on your Wi-Fi, or for 10 minutes at a time. Your PC and your Mac need to be on the same network.

  2. Step 2

    Send from Quick Share on your PC.

    Open Google's Quick Share app for Windows, add files or folders, and pick your Mac from the nearby devices.

Install in two minutes.

  1. 1 Copy it to Applications Open the .dmg and drag SQuick Share onto the Applications folder.
  2. 2 Allow it to open, once It's free and not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it: open it, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Or run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/SQuick Share.app"
  3. 3 Say yes to the local network When macOS asks to find devices on your local network, click Allow. Allow notifications and Bluetooth too, so phones find your Mac sooner.

QuestionsShort answers

Good to know.

Something else? Ask on GitHub

Is it really free?

Yes. SQuick Share is open source under the MIT License, free for personal and commercial use, with no paid tier and no account.

Do I need to install anything on my phone or PC?

Not on an Android phone: Quick Share is already there. On a Windows PC, use Google's Quick Share app for Windows. SQuick Share is the part that goes on your Mac.

My phone doesn't see my Mac. What now?

Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network) and that you clicked Allow when macOS asked about the local network. Then choose Visibility › Announce Again from the menu bar icon. On Xiaomi and Redmi phones, use Google's Quick Share, not Xiaomi Share. More help

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

SQuick Share is signed ad hoc rather than notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. The disk images are built by GitHub Actions from the public source.

Does it work with iPhone, or over the internet?

No. It's for your Mac with Android phones and Windows PCs on the same Wi-Fi. Sharing over the internet isn't supported, and iPhones already have AirDrop.
